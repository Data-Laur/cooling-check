How to read this

  • Flagged: more than 10% of the hours in the test period (about the last five months) were above the 90% band and the excess is at least 5% of the building's cooling. Excess is only the part above the band's upper edge, so it is conservative.
  • Status on 12-31-2016 looks at the most recent weeks only: "Flagged, ongoing", "Was flagged, not above band lately", or "Within expected range".
  • Can't judge means the model wasn't accurate enough on this building (or there wasn't enough clean data), so we don't guess.
  • Excess cooling is potential, not realized: a meter change or a legitimate change in use looks the same. Check the meter first.

Test period 08-01-2016 onward; the month before was used to calibrate the band; earlier data to fit the baseline.
